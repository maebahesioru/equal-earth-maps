Equal Earth Maps — turn web maps into the Equal Earth projection
----------------------------------------------------------------

Convert OpenStreetMap and GSI (Geospatial Information Authority of Japan) maps
from Web Mercator to the Equal Earth equal-area projection.

* Self-driven slippy map: pan and zoom stay in Equal Earth at any zoom level
  (tiles are fetched on demand).
* Works on openstreetmap.org and maps.gsi.go.jp.
* Toggle: browser toolbar, keyboard shortcut Ctrl+Shift+E, or the on-map pill.
* 100% local: no account, no tracking, no data sent anywhere except normal
  tile requests to the map site you are already using.

Short description:
View OpenStreetMap and 国土地理院地図 (GSI) maps in the Equal Earth
equal-area projection.

Category: Productivity / Maps & Navigation

Single purpose: Display web maps in the Equal Earth (equal-area) projection.

Permissions
- host permissions for openstreetmap.org, maps.gsi.go.jp, and their tile
  servers only (used to load map tiles for reprojection).
- storage: remember the on/off switch.

Privacy
No user data is collected, transmitted, or stored. The extension only loads
map tiles from the tile servers of the sites it runs on and processes them
locally in your browser.
